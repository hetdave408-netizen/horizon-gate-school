Academics

A curriculum that respects how children actually learn.

CBSE-aligned, inquiry-first, and built around small-group attention — from Foundational years through Senior Secondary.

Curriculum by Stage

Five stages, one continuous thread.

01

Foundational

Pre-K – Grade 2

Play-based literacy and numeracy, taught through story, movement, and structured discovery.

02

Preparatory

Grades 3 – 5

Subject specialisation begins; students take their first independent research project.

03

Middle School

Grades 6 – 8

Introduction to STEM labs, a second language, and the fabrication studio.

04

Secondary

Grades 9 – 10

CBSE Board preparation alongside elective tracks in coding, design, and the arts.

05

Senior Secondary

Grades 11 – 12

Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams with dedicated career counselling.

How We Teach

Teaching methodology

Inquiry-First

Units open with a question students can't yet answer, not a definition they must memorise.

Formative Feedback

Weekly low-stakes checks replace last-minute cramming before term exams.

Small-Group Clinics

Every student gets one weekly small-group session in their hardest subject.

Portfolio Assessment

Art, design, and STEM electives are graded on a portfolio, not a single exam.
