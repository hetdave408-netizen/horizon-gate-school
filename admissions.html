Admissions

Five steps between here and your child's first day.

Our admissions process is built to be unhurried — every family gets a campus visit and a real conversation before any paperwork.

The Process

How admission works

Fill the admission enquiry form below, or call the admissions office directly.

02

Book a school visit to walk the campus and meet a grade-level coordinator.

03

Assessment

An age-appropriate interaction for the child (no written test before Grade 3).

04

Document Verification

Submit the required documents listed below for review.

05

Offer & Enrolment

Accept the offer and complete enrolment within 10 working days.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is there an entrance test? +

No written test before Grade 3. From Grade 3 onward, it's a short, age-appropriate assessment in English and Maths, not a competitive exam.

Do you offer sibling or staff discounts? +

Yes — a 10% tuition discount applies to the second sibling enrolled, applied automatically at enrolment.

What is the class size? +

24–28 students per section, with two sections per grade.

Do you offer transport in my area? +

Our bus fleet covers 40+ routes. Share your address at the campus visit and we'll confirm the nearest stop.

Can I apply mid-year? +

Mid-year transfers are considered subject to seat availability — contact the admissions office directly.
