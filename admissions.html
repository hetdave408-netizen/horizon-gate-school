05 · Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers

Is there an entrance test? +

No written test before Grade 3. From Grade 3 onward, it's a short, age-appropriate assessment in English and Maths, not a competitive exam.

Do you offer sibling or staff discounts? +

Yes — a 10% tuition discount applies to the second sibling enrolled, applied automatically at enrolment.

What is the class size? +

24–28 students per section, with two sections per grade.

Do you offer transport in my area? +

Our bus fleet covers 40+ routes. Share your address at the campus visit and we'll confirm the nearest stop.

Can I apply mid-year? +

Mid-year transfers are considered subject to seat availability — contact the admissions office directly.
