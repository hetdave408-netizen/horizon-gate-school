Drop your real campus video here as `campus-hero.mp4` (referenced in the
homepage hero at `index.html`). Until a file exists at this path, the
hero gracefully falls back to displaying its poster image instead —
nothing breaks in the meantime.

Recommended: an MP4, H.264, under ~8MB, 1920x1080 or smaller, no audio
needed (it plays muted). A short 10-20 second loop of campus life works
best — keep it visually calm since text sits on top of it.
