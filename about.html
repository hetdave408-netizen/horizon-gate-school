Our Story

27 years of building minds, one honest classroom at a time.

Horizon Gate began in 1998 as a three-classroom school with a simple bet: that rigor and warmth aren't opposites. Today, that bet has graduated over four thousand students.

Vision

To raise students who think in public — clearly, kindly, and without waiting for permission.

We measure ourselves not by rank lists alone, but by whether a graduate can walk into an unfamiliar room and ask a good question.

Mission

Rigorous academics, real mentorship, and a campus that makes room for who a child is becoming.

Every student leaves Horizon Gate with a transcript, a portfolio, and at least one teacher who knows them well enough to write an honest letter.

From the Principal

“We’re not preparing children for a test on Friday. We’re preparing them for a life they haven’t met yet — that takes patience, and it takes rigor.”

In three decades on this campus, I’ve learned that the best classrooms are the ones where a child is allowed to be wrong out loud. That’s the culture we protect here — ambitious, kind, and unafraid of hard questions.

Dr. Anita Rao

Principal, Horizon Gate School

Why Families Stay

Why choose Horizon Gate

Experienced Faculty

82% of teachers hold a postgraduate degree in their subject; average tenure is 9 years.

Safe, Supervised Campus

CCTV-monitored campus, biometric entry, and a dedicated transport tracking app for parents.

Academic Rigor

A curriculum that goes past the textbook, with weekly problem-solving clinics in Math and Science.

Modern Infrastructure

Smart boards, STEM labs, and a fabrication studio built for grades 6 and up.

Holistic Development

40+ clubs spanning robotics, debate, theatre, and sport — every student picks at least two.

Strong Values

A values curriculum woven into daily assembly, not confined to a once-a-year workshop.

What We Hold To

Our values, in practice — not in a frame in the lobby.

Curiosity

We grade effort to ask a better question as highly as effort to find the right answer.

Integrity

Honesty is taught the way math is taught — practiced daily, not lectured once.

Resilience

Students are allowed to fail a test and still be proud of the attempt.

Community

Every grade mentors the one below it. No student walks the campus a stranger.

Since 1998

How we got here

1998

Founded

Horizon Gate opens with 3 classrooms and 40 students on a leased plot.

2006

First Batch, First Board Toppers

Our founding batch graduates; two students place in the state CBSE merit list.

2012

New 12-Acre Campus

The school relocates to its current campus, purpose-built with labs and a full sports complex.

2019

STEM & Robotics Wing

A dedicated fabrication studio and robotics lab open for grades 6 and up.

2025

Green Campus Award

Recognised for a solar-powered campus and a zero-waste cafeteria initiative.
